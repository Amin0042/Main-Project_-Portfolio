Archive

What has been gathered here is a fragment of my visual identity. It is a fragment of my shadow, cast behind me upon the uneven and endless road I have walked for many years. These works are the scattered pieces of an Iranian man who sees redemption in the arduous struggle to become better. Their witness is the eternal shadow that follows behind him, stretching beyond the horizon and into eternity.
