Archive

What has been gathered here is a fragment of my visual identity. It is a fragment of my shadow, cast behind me upon the uneven and endless road I have walked for many years. These works are the scattered pieces of an Iranian man who, trusting in a redemption freely given, still takes up the arduous struggle to become better. Their witness is the eternal shadow that follows behind him, stretching beyond the horizon and into eternity.

Personal Vault

What awaits you within this endless labyrinth is divided into three domains: Traditional, Digitized, and Posthuman. Each serves as a gateway into an unknown and boundless territory. Together, they form the identity of a curious and troubled human being who, following the counsel of Socrates, has chosen to reject the unexamined life and to venture toward the unexplored frontiers of human—and ultimately posthuman—truth.
