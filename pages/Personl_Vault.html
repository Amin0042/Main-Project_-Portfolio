Personal Vault

What awaits you within this endless labyrinth is divided into three domains: Traditional, Digitized, and Posthuman. Each serves as a gateway into an unknown and boundless territory. Together, they form the identity of a curious and troubled human being who, following the counsel of Socrates, has chosen to reject the unexamined life and to venture toward the unexplored frontiers of human—and ultimately posthuman—truth.

Traditional Vault

Here lies the work of the hand: pencil, ink, and pigment, the oldest tools entrusted to us. They are the clay from which every later form is shaped, a reminder that before we learned to build new bodies, we were first taught to see, and to give thanks for the world as it was given.

Digital Vault

Here, the hand learns a new language. Light replaces pigment and the screen becomes a canvas, yet the calling remains the same: to make as we were made, and to find, even within the circuits of this new world, traces of the Maker's fingerprint.

Cyborg Vault

The flesh was never meant to be a prison, but a beginning. Here, the machine is not a rival to the soul but another clay in the Maker's hands, and I, His apprentice, dare to shape it toward a body not yet revealed.
