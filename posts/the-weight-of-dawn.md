# The Weight Of Dawn

Morning has its own severity. It asks what remains when excuses have not yet fully awakened.

There is a kind of honesty in the first hour of the day. Before the world begins speaking loudly, the mind still belongs to itself.

## First Light

In that fragile space, even small actions feel ceremonial:

- opening the window,
- touching a cold desk,
- beginning a sentence before doubt arrives.

The dawn does not promise comfort. It offers clarity.

## What It Teaches

To rise early is not always to be productive. Sometimes it is simply to witness the world before it hardens into obligation.

That witnessing matters. It gives the day an axis.
