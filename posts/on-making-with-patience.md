# On Making With Patience

Not every meaningful work arrives with force. Some pieces grow slowly, almost in secret, asking only for consistency.

Patience is not passivity. It is disciplined return.

## The Slow Construction

A work becomes stronger when it is allowed to breathe between revisions. Distance reveals proportion. Time exposes what urgency hides.

When I revisit a draft after several days, I often find that the necessary change was not dramatic at all. It was precise.

## The Practice

To make with patience means:

1. leaving room for revision,
2. resisting the urge to publish too early,
3. allowing a work to deepen before declaring it finished.

This slowness is not weakness. It is respect for form.
