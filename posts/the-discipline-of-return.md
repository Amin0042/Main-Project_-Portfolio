# The Discipline Of Return

There are days when inspiration does not arrive, when the desk feels hostile and the page remains closed in spirit even when it lies open in front of you.

Those are the days that define devotion.

## Returning Anyway

To return to the work without spectacle is one of the most difficult forms of faith.

It means continuing when the result is uncertain. It means understanding that identity is shaped less by intensity than by recurrence.

- Return to the sketch.
- Return to the sentence.
- Return to the unfinished idea.

Over time, repetition becomes character.

## Endurance

The artist is not made only by moments of brilliance. The artist is made by returning often enough that brilliance has somewhere to land.
